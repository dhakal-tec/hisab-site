Delete your hisab account

hisab, by Bibash Dhakal

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open hisab and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email hisab.app001@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my hisab account”. Send it from the Google email address you signed in with, so we know the account is yours. We’ll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email and Google sign-in link.
  • Your expenses, bills and budget settings.
  • Groups where you’re the only member, and everything in them.

What stays

Groups you share with other people keep their expenses and payments, so everyone else’s balances still add up. Your name in those groups is replaced with “Deleted user”, and the history is no longer linked to any account.

If you used hisab without signing in, your data was only ever on your phone. Uninstall the app to remove it.
