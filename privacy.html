Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

  • You can use hisab without an account. Then everything stays on your phone.
  • If you sign in, your hisab data is backed up so you don’t lose it, and only you can see it.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling your data. hisab never connects to your bank.
  • Receipts are read on your phone. A receipt photo is only uploaded if you attach it to a group expense, and then only that group can see it.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time.

Who we are

hisab is made by Bibash Dhakal, an independent developer. In this policy “we” means the person who makes hisab. You can reach us at hisab.app001@gmail.com.

Using hisab without an account

Everything you enter (expenses, bills, your budget) is stored only on your phone. We can’t see it. Uninstalling the app removes it.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in lets hisab back up your data and lets you share groups with friends. When you sign in, Google tells us:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your profile photo
  • an ID for your Google account

We use these to sign you in and to show your name and profile photo to people in groups you join. We don’t get your Google password.

What we store when you’re signed in

  • Expenses: amount, category, note, date, and whether it was money out, money in, or a repayment.
  • Bills: name, amount, how often, and when they’re due.
  • Budget: your pay after tax, how often you’re paid, your pay day and your savings goal.
  • Groups: group names, members’ names and profile photos, shared expenses, who paid, each person’s share, payments between members, and any receipt photos you attach to a group expense.

We use this only to run hisab for you: to show your budget, back up your data and keep groups in sync.

Who can see it

  • Your expenses, bills and budget can only be read by your account.
  • Group expenses, payments and attached receipt photos can be seen by everyone in that group. Receipt photos are stored privately and deleted when the group is deleted.
  • The home-screen widget shows how much you have left until pay day. Anyone who can see your phone screen can see it, so only add it if you’re happy with that.

Things hisab does on your phone only

  • Receipt scanning: the camera is only used when you tap to scan a receipt. The text is read on your phone. For your own spending the photo is never uploaded or saved; for a group expense it is uploaded only if you choose to attach it.
  • Bill reminders: reminders are scheduled on your phone. We don’t send them from a server.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores your backed-up data and handles sign-in. Data is sent over an encrypted connection.
  • Google provides sign-in, and Google Play delivers the app.

They process data on our behalf to run hisab, under their own privacy and security terms. We don’t share your data with anyone else, and we never sell it.

What we don’t do

  • No ads and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking of how you use the app.
  • No access to your bank accounts, cards or SMS.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete an expense or bill, it disappears from the app, but a record marked “deleted” stays in your backup so your other phones know to remove it too. These records are only visible to your account, and they’re removed completely when you delete your account. If you delete your account, everything personal is deleted straight away. See Delete your account.

When you delete your account, groups you share with friends keep their history so their balances still add up, but your name is replaced with “Deleted user” and you no longer appear in the group’s member list.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change your data in the app at any time. You can delete it in the app, or email us to ask for a copy, a correction, or deletion. We’ll reply within 30 days.

Children

hisab is not made for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how hisab uses your data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app.
